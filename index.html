The two questions everyone asks

Where does my code go?

It stays on your machine — your code, your agents, your terminal all run locally. Verdict is how they keep each other posted: the transcripts and diffs your team shares sync to your own workspace, visible to nobody else, and nothing else leaves your Mac.

Do I need another AI subscription?

Not for the AI — everyone uses the Claude Code or Codex subscription they already have, or their own Anthropic or OpenAI key, and Verdict never bills for inference. What you pay Verdict for is the team: past two people or one workspace, that's the Pro plan below.
